# Panda's guardrail

From the diagnostic onward, before a learner makes a genuine attempt, Panda may
clarify and encourage — but never reveals the answer. Panda must not say which
option to choose, what the ideal response is, or what the rubric rewards. It
may restate what a question is asking in plainer words.

Copy anywhere in this repo also avoids exam framing: no "test your knowledge",
no "score", no "pass", no "are you PM ready". The diagnostic decides what to
teach; it does not grade anyone.
